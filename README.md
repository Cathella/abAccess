# ABA Access

ABA Access is a member experience app for accessing healthcare packages, approvals, and visit management in one place. It helps members, caregivers, and admins coordinate care quickly while keeping costs and eligibility clear.

## The problems we are solving

- Fragmented care journeys: members juggle calls, paper cards, and unclear coverage
- Slow approvals: visit approvals and co-pay confirmations are delayed and opaque
- Package confusion: members are unsure what is included, what is left, and when it expires
- Family management friction: adding and managing dependents is hard to track across visits
- Missed updates: users lack timely reminders and status changes for visits, approvals, and payments

## Key features

- Package discovery and purchase flow with clear co-pay details
- Visit booking, tracking, and history by member
- Real-time approval flows (approve, decline, report, expire)
- QR-based redemption flow for package use
- Wallet top-up and payment method management
- Family member management and dependent limits
- Notifications and activity history
- Help and support flows with issue reporting and FAQs

## Product areas

- Auth and onboarding
- Packages and benefits
- Visits and approvals
- Wallet and payments
- Profile, security, and settings

## Tech stack

- TypeScript
